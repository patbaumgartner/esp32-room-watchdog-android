# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Room Watchdog a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize when mistakes affect others, and learn from the
  experience.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, personal or political
attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and other
conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may edit, remove, or reject contributions, comments, commits,
code, issues, and other participation that is not aligned with this Code of
Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately through
<https://github.com/patbaumgartner/esp32-room-watchdog-android/security/advisories/new>.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The identity of a reporter will be
kept confidential except where disclosure is required by law.
